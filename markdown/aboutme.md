## What is your stance on AI?
### Full explanation
PixelIndieDev hates AI, but admits it can be a useful TOOL. He is of the opinion that AI should only be used as a tool and not as a creator. AI can be used as a tool to increase the programming speed. A lot of people misuse AI as a tool to complete full projects by themselfs, causing buggy results, poor update support and possible security risks. The one that uploads the final results should be able to explain why certain decisions where made and what code does what.

### Simple explanation
PixelIndieDev hates AI, but admits it can be a useful TOOL. He supports 'AI-assisted' (with the human, who has at least somewhat experience, doing the thinking and double checking, etc) and he does not support 'AI-generated'/'Vibe-coded' content.
